The editor of a lifetime!1

It's really one of the most pleasing piece of software that I've ever seen. They lyre of Linux, the bow of BSD, the muse of Mac.

It's difficult to explain the succor it provides, the shelter from the storm of computing that whips up winds of change faster than one can stay fast in.

It's an icon of what computing could have been if not for commercialism and succumbing so predictably to mammon. You are not a "user" of Emacs. You are an operator! So scant is the software that respects its operator, people get frustrated in the same way that when you pick up a guitar you don't sound just like the recording of Hendrix's Castles Made of Sand.

Let yourself not fall in the sea, and you'll see. You'll be a fearless warrior.

There's much more beauty there than the vinyl record bumps, or the digital magnetic signals, because you are there. The recordings are only inspiration for your creation.
